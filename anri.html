Anri Teieri (帝襟 アンリ, Teieri Anri) is a deeply dedicated and ambitious member of the Japan Football Union. Following the frustrating defeat of the Japanese National Team during the 2018 World Cup, Anri took it upon herself to propose a radical, high-stakes blueprint to the JFU executives—a visionary setup designed explicitly to forge an egoistic striker capable of securing a World Cup victory for Japan. Despite facing initial mockery from the money-minded traditionalists in the association, her absolute determination acts as the core bureaucratic foundation backing the entire Blue Lock Project.

Appearance

Anri is an exceptionally charming and attractive young woman with light, delicate features. She is defined by her distinct neck-length pinkish-orange hair, which features styled square bangs framing her forehead along with two clean braids tied neatly at the back. Her large, expressive eyes match her hair tone with a deep, rich brownish-orange shade. In her day-to-day work environment, she typically dons professional, sharp office attire and tailored secretary outfits, transitioning to formal wear or the official sleek athletic tracksuits during active Blue Lock operational stages.

Personality

Anri possesses a remarkably calm, professional demeanor balanced by an unyielding spirit. She is immensely passionate about the growth of Japanese soccer and remains completely unaffected by the stale, profit-driven mindsets of her corporate superiors. While she is genuinely polite and respectful, she will fearlessly speak her mind whenever her visions are dismissed, displaying incredible inner grit. She holds a deep capacity for empathy and directly cheers for the boys inside the facility, though her intense hyper-focus can sometimes cause her to lose track of her immediate surroundings when she gets fully caught up in a single goal.

Etymology

Her naming structure carries historical links to global football heritage:

  • Anri (アンリ): Written entirely in katakana script, her name functions as a direct Japanese transliteration of the classic French name "Henri" (meaning "ruler of the household").
  • Teieri (帝襟): Her surname directly translates to "an emperor's collar". Combined with her given name, it contextually hints at a high-status leadership figure.
  • Phonetic Origin: The phonetic rendering of her entire name serves as a brilliant, deliberate wordplay honoring legendary French forward Thierry Henry.
