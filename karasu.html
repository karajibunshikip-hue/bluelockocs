Tabito Karasu (烏 旅人, Karasu Tabito) is a highly strategic and tactical football player in the Blue Lock program. Known as the "Midfield General" or the "Assassin," he commands the pitch through an elite analytical mind, specialized upper-body shielding, and a sharp tongue. Originally a forward from Osaka, Karasu's capacity to control space and dissect structural defensive setups earned him the crucial role of Defensive Midfielder for the Blue Lock Eleven during their historical match against the Japan U-20 team, later joining the French team Paris X Gen during the Neo Egoist League.

Appearance

Karasu is a tall and lean young man with spiky dark purple hair, styled with lighter purple highlights visible at the back and a distinct, prominent pompadour-like spike pointing forward at the front. He possesses sharp blue eyes and a highly noticeable beauty mark/mole situated just under his left eye. Throughout his time in Blue Lock, Karasu’s attire shifts based on his team assignments. He wears the standard-issued black bodysuit with gray stripes during initial training phases. For the Second and Third Selections, he sported jersey #69 under Team Red and Team B respectively. Upon graduating to high-tier competitive games like the Japan U-20 showcase, he began donning the official Blue Lock uniform featuring long sleeves, athletic gloves, and jersey #6. In the Neo Egoist League, he shifts to the Paris X Gen color scheme, maintaining his preference for long sleeves and gloves on the pitch, a look he carries over into the U-20 World Cup lineup.

Personality

Karasu projects an arrogant, critical demeanor on the pitch, openly looking down on players he deems "mediocre" and targeting them relentlessly. He possesses a sharp, cynical tongue and rarely misses an opportunity to insult both allies and opponents to manipulate the match's flow. He is deeply particular about tactical precision, frequently complaining aloud when minimal structural details go wrong on the field. Despite this aggressive and blunt behavior, Karasu’s nature stems from an acute, grounded awareness of his own limits. Beneath his confident shell, he genuinely considers himself to be ordinary, using his hyper-analysis of others' structural weaknesses as a protective shield. He harbors deep respect for truly "extraordinary" talents, allowing him to establish incredible dynamic partnerships with players like Eita Otoya. His pragmatic leadership and defensive coordination showcase an underlying willingness to utilize every single psychological and systemic advantage at his disposal to secure a win.

Abilities

Operating as a premier defensive and transition midfielder, Karasu acts as the structural anchor of his team, turning clean data retrieval into explosive offense.

  • Sharp Analytical Ability: Karasu diagnoses the opposition to actively pinpoint their weakest link, orchestrating plays directly into that weak point to disrupt their entire system. This ability makes him an ideal transition hub between defensive stops and attacking drives.
  • Exquisite Ball Control & Hand-Fighting: By combining tactical awareness with a brilliant use of his hands and upper body, Karasu holds off pressing defenders. He pins his arms out to control structural spacing, rendering opponents incapable of reaching the ball while he executes devastating feints.
  • Metavision: Achieved by maximizing his central and peripheral fields of vision, Karasu absorbs constant positional data across the grass to read incoming high-tier plays, famously demonstrated by cutting off complex offensive volleys.
  • Flow State: When fully immersed in his competitive element, Karasu completely adapts his positioning to act as a physical pivot, lowering his frame to absorb multi-player pressure and carve out millimeter-precise paths for his teammates.

Trivia

  • His surname "Karasu" translates directly to "crow" in Japanese, which references his overall appearance, color scheme, and sharp profile.
  • He was born and raised in Osaka, which accounts for his distinct, expressive regional dialect.
  • He shares a deep history with Yo Hiori, having played alongside him on the same youth team in Osaka.
  • According to the official Egoist Bible, Karasu ironically harbors a deep fear of the water and lists swimming as his worst subject.
  • His signature hobby is observing, analyzing, and assessing human behavior.
  • His favorite subjects in school are Japanese Literature, Chemistry, and World History.
  • He admits to having a voice fetish, and his ideal preference is a girl who is both smart and strong.
  • He washes his hair first when taking a bath to thoroughly scrub out the heavy amount of styling wax required for his pompadour spike.
  • He dislikes receiving surprise presents because he despises the social expectation of being forced to act happy on cue.
  • He receives an average of 6.5 hours of sleep per night.
  • If given 100 million yen, his practical mind would immediately lead him to invest the entire sum into index funds.
  • By the end of the Neo Egoist League tournament, he secured a major professional valuation of ¥55,000,000 from France's Paris X Gen.
