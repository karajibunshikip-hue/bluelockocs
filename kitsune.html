Kitsune Seiryu (狐 清流, Kitsune Seiryu) is a high-caliber forward hailing from Tokyo who entered the Blue Lock project alongside his classmates from Hakuho High School. Standing at an imposing 193 cm, Seiryu possesses a unique, fluid playstyle that combines incredible physical leverage with razor-sharp unpredictability. Despite portraying himself as detached, cold, and arrogant, his tactical dominance on the pitch has earned him a prominent role within Blue Lock's highest tiers, currently competing under the Neo Egoist League for Germany's Bastard Munchen.

Appearance

Kitsune Seiryu is a strikingly elegant character whose design perfectly captures a cold, predatory grace. His most captivating feature is his vivid, electric-blue eyes, which possess sharp, slit pupils resembling those of a mythical dragon. These piercing eyes are heavily framed by dark lashes and highlighted with unique red under-eye markings that contrast intensely with his exceptionally pale skin and sharp, aristocratic jawline. His expression remains consistently detached and unbothered, giving him an air of absolute calm and quiet confidence regardless of his environment. His hair is a masterful showcase of style, featuring an asymmetrical undercut where long, fluid layers of silver-white sweep dramatically over one side of his face. The under-layers and the tips of his hair fade into a deep, dark teal-blue, culminating in a sleek, low ponytail that rests over his shoulder. This striking color scheme is complemented by an intricate, artistic blue tattoo of coiling, scale-like patterns winding up the left side of his neck, as well as an array of sharp black studs, hoops, and cuffs decorating his ears. He also wears glossy black nail polish, adding another layer of meticulous edge to his appearance. On the soccer pitch, his style remains impeccably sharp, where he wears a high-collared blue athletic jersey that perfectly frames his neck tattoo and draws full focus to his striking silver hair.

Personality

Seiryu often projects an icy, arrogant persona, carrying himself with the absolute certainty of an elite athlete who fiercely detests losing. He is fiercely independent and blunt, rarely sugarcoating his thoughts, which causes others to view him as unapproachable. However, this cold exterior conceals an intensely sensitive side; Seiryu deeply struggles with feelings of isolation, admitting that it feels terrible when people ignore him. He holds a massive amount of respect for his father, who serves as his main footballing inspiration, creating a sharp contrast against his otherwise dismissive attitude toward authority and expectations. While he values his personal space, he possesses a quiet longing for deep, meaningful connections with the people he loves most.

Abilities

Seiryu dominates the field using his massive frame and exceptional center of gravity to execute unpredictable movements that leave defenders completely off-balance.

  • Centrifugal Turning: Leveraging his 193 cm height and exceptional foot size (29 cm), Seiryu can execute high-speed, sharp-angled spins. By anchoring his body weight perfectly, he creates extreme centrifugal force to break past tight defensive lines smoothly, mimicking the winding movement of a dragon.
  • Calculated Rebounds: Despite claiming he hates math and calculating, Seiryu possesses a brilliant spatial instinct. He relies on kinetic tracking to accurately predict where the ball will ricochet off players or the post, allowing him to react instantly ahead of any other forward to seize loose balls.

Trivia

  • He attends Hakuho High School, the same prestigious institution attended by Seishiro Nagi and Reo Mikage.
  • His favorite foods are Sushi and Lemon Tea.
  • His favorite colors are Black and Sea Blue, which perfectly match his aesthetic and jersey styling.
  • His favorite animals are snakes and dragons, which matches the scale-like coiling tattoo running up his neck.
  • His favorite season is Winter, stating that it is the perfect cozy time to watch his pets sleep.
  • He loves spending his holidays streaming games, drawing, or traveling abroad.
  • His best academic subjects are Arts and Foreign Languages, whereas he absolutely detests Math because he hates complex calculations.
  • If he received ¥100M, he would invest a portion into his family's business and use the rest for personal use.
  • When asked what he would do on the last day of Earth, he softened and answered, "I want to be with the person I love the most."
  • He admits that his greatest weakness is his health; he feels terrible when hit with a fever, especially because it renders him unable to go outside.
  • His deep sleep schedule requires exactly 9 hours of rest to maintain his massive physical stamina.
  • He has a snake as a pet named "Shoko".
