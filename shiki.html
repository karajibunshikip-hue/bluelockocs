Shiki Karajibun (死希 から自分, Karajibun Shiki) is a prodigious football player recognized as the best player internationally. A member of the New Generation World XI, he is the heir to a major Computer Science company owned by his mother. After a successful stint with PERSIB in Indonesia, he entered the Blue Lock program as a foundational disruptor. He is a self-proclaimed "Master of None," a title that hides his reality as a polymathic architect of the pitch.

Appearance

Shiki Karajibun stands at an average height of 173 cm and possesses a sleek, high-efficiency athletic build. Due to chronic anemia, his complexion is notably pale and ghostly, which contrasts sharply with his vibrant hair and eyes. He has a medium-length wolf cut in a deep Wine Red or Burgundy shade, featuring sharp, jagged layers that graze his shoulders and sweep past his nape. A prominent ahoge or cowlick sits atop his head, serving as a signature "glitch" in his otherwise structured design. Shiki’s most striking feature is his electric Hot Pink eyes, which often appear half-lidded or heavy with under-eye bags, giving him the appearance of a sleep-deprived intellectual. His gaze is frequently described as "overloaded" because his Hyperphantasia causes him to constantly process 3D simulations in his mind. Physically, he is a rare "Zero-Sum" athlete with perfect bodily symmetry, being fully ambidextrous and ambipedal. He maintains a permanent deadpan expression and carries a certain "deadpan elegance" in his style, often appearing indifferent to his own aesthetic while naturally maintaining it.

Personality

He is clinical, blunt, and deeply intellectual individual. He possesses a "Zero-Sum" mindset, viewing the world through the lens of an Architect. He finds social constructs like homophobia and sexism "mathematically inefficient" and "cringe," famously stating they make him "want to die." He is an Omnist, meaning he recognizes and respects the "Universal Logic" in all religions without subscribing to one.

Abilities

  • Hyper-Rendering: Using Hyperphantasia, he "watches" the match in his head before it happens.
  • Future Collapse: He identifies the "load-bearing" player to trigger a structural failure in the defense.
  • Ambi-Dominance: He is fully ambidextrous and ambipedal, making his movements impossible to predict.
